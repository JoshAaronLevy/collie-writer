The Lantern Library — fictional source note

This source, its author, and the town below are invented for Collie Writer's tutorial. It is not evidence about a real place.

In the imaginary town of Bellweather, volunteers converted a closed train depot into a small public reading room. The room opened three afternoons each week. Residents first used it to exchange books, then added a table for local history notes.

The story leaves an open research question: did access to the reading room change how residents shared neighborhood records? A single source note cannot answer that question. Compare accounts, record what each supports or challenges, and keep uncertainties visible.
