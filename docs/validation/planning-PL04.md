## Corpus, coverage and request lifecycle

`shared/planning-conversation.ts`, worker `planning-corpus.ts` and `planning-conversation.ts` define the missing planning contract. They use canonical material IDs and existing note/source/excerpt/chat tables, not a second library, search service or job framework.

- Keyset pages of 100 material records enumerate the whole selected scope independently of visible cards and recency. Chapter scope includes direct and parent assignments, not sibling sections. Active state, editorial Set aside, saved context exclusions, do-not-recall imports, message visibility/eligibility and current source-version/attachment/excerpt rules remain authoritative. Captured chat notes also respect their original chat’s exclusion. Excluded manuscript metadata is removed from the bounded structural overview. No hidden manuscript body is included.
- Notes use canonical plain text; source metadata is explicitly labeled metadata, and extracted pages retain version/page/hash identity. Native completed exchanges and eligible imported user/assistant messages retain message/revision/sequence/role identity. Imported text uses hash-checked managed originals through `OriginalTranscriptText`, independently of old recall windows. Individual reads are bounded by existing original-reader limits. Exact UTF-16 ranges avoid splitting surrogate pairs.
- A frozen analysis snapshot stores references, ranges, revisions, scope, prompt, structural purpose and coverage counts, without duplicating original bodies. Up to 20,000 ranges are admitted per snapshot, with a 32 MiB body limit and 1,000 retained analyses per project. Ranges are at most 12,000 units; sequential batches contain at most ten ranges and 16,000 original-text units. Material beyond that snapshot bound, unreadable items and partially readable items are explicitly counted. Repair unavailable originals or narrow the scope and Send again when a hard collection bound is reached.
- Ordinary questions rank the inventory by title, inspect at most 256 candidate chunks locally and provide up to eight relevant original passages within 16,000 text units. This is expressly a selection, never whole-project coverage. Collection analysis processes eligible batches in order, maintaining a cumulative AI digest of at most 6,000 units. Each protected summary retains its exact new ranges and previous producing attempt. The final answer receives that digest and relevant original passages from the analyzed portion. Compression is lossy; it is not factual verification or verbatim evidence.
- A Send/Continue action freezes scope and selected account/model for at most ten sequential preparation requests plus one answer, using existing reviewed direct text/research operations. The existing request/context/output bounds and ten-minute wait per preparation remain. No automatic repair or retry request is sent. Oversized, stopped, failed or uncertain summaries stay readable but do not advance coverage. Only explicit Continue can start another bounded run.
- Completed summaries are reused by exact prefix digest and analysis contract. Changing an earlier input invalidates that prefix and all dependent cumulative summaries; an unchanged prefix remains reusable. Authoritative snapshot comparison before review/append refuses stale material. Current checks reuse known imported range boundaries without repeatedly decoding unchanged original files; actual supplied passages still resolve their exact managed originals. Sources and ranges remain navigable through retained transcript, note, source and excerpt owners.
- Scope, completion counts and progress survive restart and independent copy. Opening, Save, importing, changing scope and recovery never dispatch work. Existing protected attempts, binding receipts, acknowledgment and original-project reconciliation remain the authority. Continue adopts retained completed progress; incomplete work requires a fresh explicit attempt. No provider credentials or live execution grant are stored in a portable snapshot.
