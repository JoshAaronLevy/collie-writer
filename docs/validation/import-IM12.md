## Format losses and deliberate boundaries

- Supported intake: JSON/CSL-JSON, UTF-8 text/Markdown, BibTeX and RIS. No PDF/DOCX/HTML/ZIP/OCR/media reader or project-container import. Unknown or malformed JSON layouts may remain retained/unclassified/unsupported; model interpretation cannot supply missing local identities or text authority.
- Only selected files are available. Embedded paths and URLs remain inert; imported references do not fetch articles, attach missing media, verify publications or create inspected excerpts. Bibliography attribution/grades remain original claims. Rejected/search candidates and unsupported widgets/media do not become active cited evidence.
- Raw current-node parent chains and correlated arrays establish ordering; ambiguous/missing identity or unsupported alternate paths require exclusion/resolution. Original role, metadata, variants and timestamps remain evidence; missing precision/time zone stays unknown. Internal reasoning/hidden bodies remain in whole originals but are not ordinary transcript/context/analysis.
- Notes preserve literal supported conversion with losses; Markdown is not executed HTML. Chat prose is not automatically authored notes. Unknown fields/oversized bibliographic metadata remain at their original locators; corrected mappings are explicit user choices.
- Analysis outputs propose bounded structured additions/title suggestions, never regenerate original transcripts. It can take several explicit finite grants to cover the full collection. Invalid/partial output is retained as unsuccessful and cannot be silently repaired or accepted.
- Whole originals retain excluded/private bytes and older variants. No automatic purge or dependency-blind batch undo. Repeated/changed external identities do not overwrite or synchronize native records. A partially accepted batch is final; excluded leftovers need another batch.
- Bounded imported context may omit long or older originals (including the snapshot's longest message); full transcript remains readable. Summary/proposal/provider suitability requires user observation. Exported chat/bibliography/manuscript formats do not carry every project-only provenance/variant/receipt/grade/link; `.collie` is the portable project artifact.
