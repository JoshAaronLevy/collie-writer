# PS03 storage advice and space context

October 5, 2026. Addendum to the [inventory contract](storage-inventory.md). SQL/minimum reader **13**, AST/archive **1**, compilation **3**, snapshot/file journals, export reports and AI recovery formats remain unchanged.

## Measurement and advice

The validated transient inventory reply now includes up to 64 volume readings, up to 128 condition readings and an omitted-condition count. Conditions are calculated from **all measured groups**, before group pagination. Threshold crossings receive priority in the bounded summary, followed by volume/overall-cache conditions. Omission never clears remembered advice.

`STORAGE_ADVICE` in `src/shared/storage-advice.ts` owns the tunable product defaults: 500 MiB project search cache, 2 GiB overall search cache, 5 GiB local project data, and available volume space below the greater of 5 GiB or 10% of total capacity. These are advice thresholds, independent of operation admission. The local-project reading includes workspace files and already attributed AI records; recorded external files and unassigned shared data remain separate, not silently attributed to a project. File lengths are still not allocated or reclaimable bytes.

Size conditions activate at their threshold and clear only below 80% with a complete, sufficiently known reading. Unknown working entries prevent project-size/cache resolution; unclassified content also prevents asserting complete cache classification. Partial lower bounds can establish a crossing, but cannot establish a size recovery. Volume conditions activate below their threshold and clear at 125%; a known capacity reading is independent of completeness of file enumeration. Between/unknown conditions retain the earlier latch. Neither absent scopes nor omitted conditions establish recovery.

Capacity uses metadata-only `statfs` on already observed root/category directories, under the scanner's existing time/cancellation/device bounds. Ancestors, directory identity and device are checked around the query; unsafe/unavailable/inconsistent/unsafe-integer readings stay null. No external content or archive is opened. The available count uses `bavail`, not bytes calculated from project files. Readings have their own timestamps and may become stale immediately. Empty root volumes can be reported; undetected/unavailable devices cannot be inferred from another volume's free space.

Per-scan Volume labels remain presentation only. A derived local device key makes snoozes consistent when the same observed device receives a different label in a later scan. It is not a file hash, portable identity, filesystem grant or account identifier. Device keys, paths, readings and error context are not included in support diagnostics; the existing support preview retains only bounded error codes.

## Notice preference and presentation

`collie.storage-notices.v1` is a device-local renderer-profile preference with exact `{ version: 1, entries }`. Each entry has exact `key`, `active`, `dismissed`, `until` and `seen` fields: a validated condition key, two booleans and nonnegative safe millisecond timestamps. There are at most 128 entries and 40,000 characters on read. Snoozes last 24 hours; entries expire after 30 days without measurements. Active/recent choices receive retention priority. Capacity/key bounds may cause older choices to be forgotten; they never affect project data or dispatch authority. Invalid data uses fresh measurements with feedback. Failed writes keep the session choice with feedback.

The retained provider accepts terminal/partial reports, not every running poll. Measure/Refresh remains explicit: no startup, keystroke, background library scan or hidden polling was added. A passive workspace summary survives navigation; no modal, typing interruption or close override is introduced. The Data view owns full notices, snooze/dismiss/restore actions and measurement times. Review opens the matching page/project disclosure/search row or volume reading. Focus waits for the visible region, modal exit, foreground visibility and composition completion; stale review requests cannot target a replacement scan. No Clear cache/content-removal control exists.

## Space checks and reservations

Existing `requireSpace` preflights retain their estimates and 64 MiB margin, now with stable directory/device checks and explicit unknown-capacity failure. Measurable export candidate/destination/asset writes also check their known output bytes. Snapshot, file, export and ordinary queued worker operations each own an asynchronous budget until their work settles. Device-keyed peak estimates are not summed repeatedly for the same job. Admission compares fresh available space with its current requested bytes/margin plus other cooperating worker jobs' estimates; working and destination devices remain separate. Save's existing combined same-volume estimate remains intact.

Peak estimates are conservative: bytes already written may remain reserved until settlement. These are not OS reservations, promises of successful writes, estimates for every future allocation, or reservations against other processes, native PDF staging, main-process AI/profile writes or external applications. Unknown capacity fails a preflight instead of being admitted. Native I/O/other writers can still cause later failure.

PS08's [compression-budget addendum](archive-compression-v1.md) updates archive creation to a worst-case deflated/stored candidate plus full expanded inspection. Save/Backup publication budgets two full expanded inspections, adding actual destination staging/previous-copy lengths on a shared device. A compressed candidate's physical length never substitutes for its expanded validation copies. Expanded limits and the existing per-device ownership/margin remain; smaller saved files do not establish smaller working-space requirements or authorize cache/content deletion.

`SpaceIssue` is validated transient context: operation, location, required/available bytes or null, other-worker reserved bytes, and insufficient/unknown/write-failed reason. Project failures can carry it with a deterministically rendered message; file-job views have an optional field. Snapshot failures retain up to 64 contexts in memory and propagate them through existing finish/retry. Export contexts are kept separately in memory (64 jobs, up to four issues per job), attached only to views and explicitly excluded from persistent reports. Older durable failures reopen without context and say the additional bytes/failing volume are unknown. Native write paths are used when safely available; a raw write failure never borrows the location/estimate of an unrelated earlier preflight.

The original error code, exact operation IDs, draft/buffer ownership, uncertain replies, journal phases, retained candidates, close/access guards, and recovery actions remain authoritative. Advice and diagnostic context cannot authorize retry with a new identity, discard a buffer, clear cache, remove originals or relax protection. Error messages point to Data and recovery and existing Save As/Backup; another destination still needs working space.
